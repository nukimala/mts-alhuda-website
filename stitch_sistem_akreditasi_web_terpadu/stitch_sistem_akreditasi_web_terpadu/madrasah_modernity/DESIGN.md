## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-looking Islamic educational institution: rigorous, grounded, and dignified, yet intrinsically warm and nurturing. It reconciles traditional values with a contemporary, high-performing academic interface.

### Aesthetic Persona
- **Warm Authority:** Institutional reliability paired with empathetic pastoral care. It avoids sterile corporate bureaucracy while retaining institutional discipline.
- **Organic Modernism:** A digital environment inspired by growth, sanctuary, and clarity. Deep verdant greens evoke heritage, nature, and peaceful academic immersion.
- **Approachability:** Dual-audience calibration ensures parents and younger students experience an intuitive, friendly touchpoint, while faculty and administrators encounter an efficient, modular data system.

### Style Archetype
The style blends **Modern Organic Tactility** with **Structured Cleanliness**:
- Soft tinted surfaces (`#E8F5EC`, `#DCEFE2`) prevent stark starkness and reduce eye strain during extended portal usage.
- High-order rounded edges (`rounded-xl`) soften administrative structures without sliding into overly playful or childish shapes.
- Precise typography balances welcoming geometry with clear information hierarchy.

## Colors

The palette draws its strength from a spectrum of botanical and forest greens, providing visual calm and dignified authority suited for an educational environment.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0B3D2E` - Deep Forest Green):** The institutional core. Used for primary navigation bars, dominant action buttons, core brand markers, and high-emphasis headings.
- **Secondary (`#1E7145` - Lush Emerald):** The functional complement. Applied to active states, secondary badges, section titles, and structural graphic elements.
- **Tertiary (`#219653` - Vibrant Green):** The interactive signal. Used for interactive focus rings, key success notifications, verification badges, and high-priority action affordances.
- **Soft Tint Containers:**
  - **Mint Surface (`#E8F5EC`):** Page canvas accents, subtle selection tiles, and low-contrast tag fills.
  - **Subtle Mint Border & Container (`#DCEFE2`):** Card enclosures, tab group tracks, table header fills, and passive divider strokes.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - **Canvas Base:** `#FBFDFB` (Warm pure white with a micro-drop of green undertone).
  - **Surface Container:** `#F2F8F4`.
  - **Text Primary (`#1D2A24`):** High-contrast, slate-tinted dark forest neutral for long-form reading comfort.
  - **Text Secondary (`#4A6356`):** Muted botanical slate for metadata, supporting labels, and subheadings.

### Hierarchy & Application
- Administrative views use `#FBFDFB` surfaces with `#DCEFE2` structural borders to maximize table legibility.
- Student & Parent views feature `#E8F5EC` card containers to project hospitality and visual comfort.
- Critical feedback patterns (destructive/alerts) employ a grounded terracotta (`#C84B31`) to harmonize naturally alongside deep forest green without visual jarring.

## Typography

The design system exclusively utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to ensure geometric balance, modern cleanliness, and warm approachability. Its humanized terminal angles provide friendliness for family-facing portals, while its crisp metric proportions maintain strict tabular order in gradebooks and records.

### Implementation Rules
- **Headers & Identity:** `display` and `headline-lg` are deployed for welcoming campus announcements, portal homepages, and top-level dashboard metrics. Always set in primary `#0B3D2E` with tighter letter-spacing for refined presence.
- **Academic & Operational Data:** Data tables, attendance logs, and administrative forms rely strictly on `body-md` and `label-md` to maintain dense information readability without horizontal overcrowding.
- **Numerals & Metrics:** Arabic numbers within student report cards and grading scales inherit the natural tabular figures of Plus Jakarta Sans, keeping lists and schedules vertically aligned.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured layout system built on an 8px base rhythm accommodates administrative information density and open, welcoming landing spaces.

### Grid Foundations
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `2rem` outer page margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Administrative dashboards deploy a fixed 260px collapsible sidebar navigation, with the main content area occupying the fluid remaining space (max-width capped at 1440px for comfortable reading spans).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Sidebars collapse to icon-only rails or slide-over sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer margin and `0.75rem` gutters. Complex data tables degrade gracefully to vertical stacked cards.

### Spacing Cadence
- **Component Tightness (`space-xs`, `space-sm`):** Internal component padding (chips, badge icons, nested list items, and input margins).
- **Component Separation (`space-md`, `space-lg`):** Gap spacing between cards, form fields, and table sections.
- **Section Breaks (`space-xl`):** Major page divisions, separating academic calendars from announcements and summary widgets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layer stacking** combined with **subtle tinted ambient diffusion**, avoiding harsh dropped shadows in favor of a clean, soft academic presence.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the main screen viewport (`#FBFDFB`).
- **Level 1 (Surface Containers):** Applied to standard content cards, data tables, and academic feeds. Constructed using solid white (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` structural outline in `#DCEFE2`. Zero blur shadow is necessary at this level, establishing clean, modern boundaries.
- **Level 2 (Raised Modals & Dropdowns):** Floating elements, user profile flyouts, and modal dialogues use white backgrounds combined with a green-tinted ambient drop shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(11, 61, 46, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(11, 61, 46, 0.04);`
- **Level 3 (Toasts & Overlays):** Interactive floating action bars and system toasts:
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 32px -6px rgba(11, 61, 46, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(11, 61, 46, 0.06);`

### Border System
Borders remain essential to retain administrative rigor. Key structural cards use a precise `1px solid #DCEFE2`. Hover states on actionable cards transition the border softly to `#219653` accompanied by a micro-elevation lift of `-2px`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a unified, inviting curvature. Standard elements apply the `roundedness: 2` scale, rendering interfaces approachable to families while preserving structural discipline.

### Corner Curvature Rules
- **Base Components (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Input fields, table row action badges, tags, and internal nested indicators.
- **Core Cards & Modules (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / 16px):** Standard dashboard widgets, assignment cards, grade summary sheets, and modal frames.
- **Hero Banners & System Hubs (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / 24px):** Major dashboard header summaries, announcement cards, and mobile bottom sheets.
- **Pill Targets (`9999px`):** Status chips, circular user avatars, quick-filter tab groups, and notification counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with `#0B3D2E`, label in white, `rounded-lg` (12px). Height: 44px (touch target compliant). Hover shifts background to `#1E7145`. Active state scales subtly to `0.98`.
- **Secondary:** Filled with `#E8F5EC`, label in `#0B3D2E`, border: `1px solid #DCEFE2`. Hover fills to `#DCEFE2`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, label in `#1E7145`. Hover applies a `#E8F5EC` wash.

### Cards
- **Base Card:** Solid `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #DCEFE2`, `rounded-xl` (24px) border radius, padded with `1.5rem`.
- **Interactive Academic Card:** Features a soft vertical accent pill (4px wide) on the left margin colored in `#219653` for active class sessions or upcoming events.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 44px, background `#FFFFFF`, border: `1px solid #DCEFE2`, corner radius: `0.5rem` (8px). Placeholder text in `#4A6356`. Focus state applies a crisp `2px` ring in `#219653` with zero canvas bleed.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `20px x 20px`, border `1.5px solid #1E7145`. Active selection fills `#0B3D2E` with a bold white check mark.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Height 28px, pill-shaped (`9999px`), padding: `0 12px`.
  - *Active / Present / Approved:* `#E8F5EC` background with `#1E7145` text and a `#219653` dot indicator.
  - *Pending / Review:* Soft amber tint with olive-brown text.
  - *Late / Warning:* Soft terracotta wash with `#C84B31` text.

### Lists & Academic Tables
- **Header Rows:** Filled with `#E8F5EC`, text set in `label-sm` uppercase, color `#1D2A24`.
- **Row Styling:** Minimum height 52px. Hover triggers `#F2F8F4` background transition. Divided with `1px solid #DCEFE2`.

### Madrasah-Specific Components
- **Prayer & Class Schedule Widget:** Integrated schedule strip displaying five daily prayers and school periods with `#E8F5EC` time markers and a `#219653` current-session indicator.
- **Memorization & Academic Progress Bar:** Multi-segment progress bar with `#DCEFE2` track fill, completed milestones filled in `#1E7145`, and current target marked with `#219653`.
- **Parent-Teacher Communication Snippet:** Soft `#F2F8F4` enclosure with a small green ribbon badge (`#1E7145`) highlighting verified faculty messages.
